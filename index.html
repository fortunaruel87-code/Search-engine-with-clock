About Digital Clock by Fortune

Digital Clock by Fortune is a simple and modern digital clock website designed to provide an easy way to check the current time.

Features

  • 🕐 Digital Clock – Displays the current time in real time.
  • 12 HR / 24 HR – Switch between 12-hour and 24-hour formats.
  • 🎨 Theme Button – Allows customization of the website appearance.
  • 🔎 Google Search – Search Google directly from the website.
  • 📱 Responsive Design – Designed to work on computers, tablets, and mobile phones.

About the Creator

Digital Clock by Fortune is created by Fortune, with the goal of making a simple, clean, and useful digital clock that is easy for everyone to use.
